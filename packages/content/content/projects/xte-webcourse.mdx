HTWK Leipzig runs a technical-English course, e-Xplore Technical English, on a platform that had stopped working for its students: weak on mobile, unreliable sign-in, low usage. The university commissioned a replacement.

We built it as an external contractor under fixed-price offers, delivered in increments the faculty requested as their material grew: course management, fifteen interaction types for exercises, C-tests with instant grading, a spaced-repetition flashcard trainer, progress tracking, certificates, analytics for teachers and admin tooling. The last increment added an AI agent that drafts new exercises from source texts, so the teaching staff generate material instead of typing it.

Stack: a React and TypeScript frontend, a Kotlin backend on Spring, university single sign-on via SAML2 plus OAuth2 for external users. The platform is in production use at HTWK Leipzig.
